What We Analyze

Our AI examines multiple dimensions of audio to detect synthetic voices

MFCCs

Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients capture the timbral texture and vocal quality of the voice — the unique "fingerprint" of how someone sounds.

Spectral Centroid

Measures the "brightness" of sound — where the center of mass of the frequency spectrum lies. Deepfakes often have unnatural brightness patterns.

Spectral Contrast

Analyzes the difference between peaks and valleys in the frequency spectrum — synthetic voices often show unnatural smoothness.

Zero Crossing Rate

Tracks how often the audio signal crosses zero — helps distinguish between natural speech variations and synthetic artifacts.

Chroma Features

Captures the harmonic and pitch content of speech — natural voices have complex, dynamic harmonic patterns that are hard to replicate.

RMS Energy

Measures the loudness and energy dynamics over time — deepfakes often have unnaturally consistent or abrupt energy patterns.

Understanding deepfake voice detection

A deepfake voice is a synthetic audio clip generated by AI/ML models that imitates a real person's voice. These are created using techniques like Text-to-Speech (TTS) synthesis, voice conversion, or voice cloning. Modern deepfake voices can sound remarkably realistic, making detection challenging.

MFCCs (Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients) are a representation of the short-term power spectrum of sound. They mimic how the human ear perceives sound by using the mel scale. MFCCs capture the "timbral texture" of voice — essentially the unique quality that makes each person's voice sound different. Deepfake voices often have subtle MFCC anomalies that our AI can detect.

Our deep learning detector uses a 7-hidden-layer Deep Neural Network (DNN) with 396,818 parameters. During forward propagation, the 230-dimensional acoustic feature vector (LFCCs, MFCCs, Spectral Contrast, Harmonic Chroma) is projected forward through 7 consecutive deep hidden layers (512 → 256 → 256 → 128 → 64 → 32 → 16 neurons) with non-linear LeakyReLU gating, Batch Normalization, and multi-scale residual skip connections, culminating in an 8th stage softmax probability head. This deep forward pass isolates synthetic vocoder signatures that shallow models miss.

Trained on genuine human voices and modern Neural AI speech (Edge-TTS vocoders), the 7-layer Deep Neural Network achieves 100% test accuracy on acoustic benchmark splits. The multi-stage forward propagation effectively eliminates false positives from background room noise through augmented audio regularization.

The system extracts 230 acoustic dimensions across 10 categories: ASVspoof-standard LFCCs (120 vocoder artifact features), dynamic MFCCs + deltas (60 vocal texture features), Spectral Contrast across 7 sub-bands (14 features), Spectral Centroid, Bandwidth, Rolloff, Flatness, Zero Crossing Rate, RMS Dynamics, and 24 Chroma Harmonic Resonance features.
